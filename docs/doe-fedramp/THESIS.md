## One-sentence thesis

hOUR Chain’s patterns for **verifiable provenance, durable evidence, access control, and attributable settlement** are creator-rights infrastructure today; the same *record-integrity* metaphors could matter in DOE-adjacent conversations about **high-integrity or regulated records** — if and only when a named lab or program partner wants that class of software, and without claiming nuclear engineering, fuel-cycle operations, or dual-use capability Witching Hour does not have.

---

## What Witching Hour actually builds

hOUR Chain is **creator-rights infrastructure**: provenance, access, and settlement for music and other recorded creative work. It models:

- Creator identity and works / recordings  
- Contributor and rights-split graphs  
- Signed provenance events  
- Access credentials and agent permissions  
- Settlement instructions  

So hours behind creative work become attributable, licensable, and auditable rather than trapped in private chats and cloud folders.

**Phase 1 stack (honest scope):** Base (EVM settlement + commitments), Lightning/Solana adapters, Filecoin durable evidence (Open Grant #2182 track), post-quantum-native design so signature suites stay replaceable. Clients (Phigit OS, Witching Hour App, etc.) consume the protocol; grant-funded Filecoin work stays in the public `hour-chain-filecoin` boundary.

This is software and evidence design — **not** nuclear facilities, enrichment, reprocessing, HEU handling, or weapons-related work.

---

## Why DOE interest exists (honest framing)

Fletcher’s deep reading on **highly enriched uranium (HEU)** and the **nuclear fuel cycle** is treated as **domain literacy** for partnership conversations: vocabulary, institutional map, and enough context to ask intelligent questions and avoid fantasy pitches.

It is **not**:

- A claim that Witching Hour designs reactors, fuel, enrichment, or safeguards systems  
- A hiring path into DOE as a Research Associate (this pack explicitly rejects that track)  
- A dual-use, weaponization, or “heavier than HEU” engineering offering  
- Permission to file forms meant for civil nuclear technology vendors as if Witching Hour were one  

**Honest bridge:** regulated and high-integrity environments care about *who attested what, when; what evidence is durable and retrievable; who may access it; and how disputes settle*. Creator-rights systems already force those questions. Transfer the **metaphor** (provenance graphs, signed events, evidence bundles, access credentials, auditability) only where it remains true. Do not transfer metaphors that imply nuclear process knowledge, facility design, or material accounting capability.

## Where partnership *might* fit (hypothesis, not claims)

Possible conversation surfaces — only with a named counterpart and an honest fit:

| Pattern from hOUR Chain | Possible regulated-records metaphor | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signed provenance events | Attestable event logs for high-integrity workflows | Not “nuclear material accounting” unless a lab partner defines that problem and owns the domain |
| Durable, retrievable evidence (Filecoin track) | Long-lived evidentiary storage with integrity checks | Not classified storage; not claiming FedRAMP/ATO |
| Access credentials / agent permissions | Controlled release of records to authorized parties | Not clearance systems; not insider-threat products |
| Rights-split / settlement graphs | Multi-party attributable obligations | Commercial/creator firstly; federal only if a buyer asks |

Public DOE partnership *mechanisms* (CRADA, partnership intermediaries, SBIR/STTR, lab tech-transfer) exist for U.S. small businesses and labs to collaborate. They are researched in `NEXT-30-DAYS.md`. **None of them are claimed as existing Witching Hour relationships.**

## Partnership posture (what “yes” looks like)

1. **Filecoin / near-term revenue first.** Open Grant #2182 and Mirrorizm/sites stay the money ladder; DOE is a parallel *relationship* track with near-zero spend.  
2. **Partnership, not employment.** Seek collaborative R&D, vouchers, or topic-aligned SBIR *only if* eligibility and topic fit are real — not a Research Associate hire.  
3. **Named counterpart before compliance spend.** FedRAMP deferred until a named federal buyer (or clear 20x Program path tied to a real use case). See `FEDRAMP.md`.  
4. **Literacy without impersonation.** Read HEU/fuel-cycle material to understand stakes and institutions; speak as a software/evidence team seeking fit, not as a nuclear OEM.  
5. **Export-control awareness.** Civil nuclear tech assistance is regulated (e.g. 10 CFR Part 810). Witching Hour’s current product is creator-rights software; still, any future nuclear-*adjacent* technical discussion with foreign persons or controlled tech requires legal awareness *before* sharing. See open questions.
