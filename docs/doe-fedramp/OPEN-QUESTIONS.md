## Notes for Fletcher (jargon)

**Personnel clearance** — Background investigation so a *person* can see classified information. Different from company registrations (SAM, JCP) and from FedRAMP (cloud product authorization).

**Export control** — U.S. rules (ITAR/EAR and related) on sharing certain tech, data, or defense/nuclear-adjacent know-how with foreign persons or abroad. “Process” here means: before any nuclear-adjacent technical deep-dive with foreign collaborators, we write down what is shared, with whom, and whether a license is needed. For now the answer is simply: don’t start that work.

**Designate** — Someone else you appoint to fill or sign forms for you. You chose yourself only.

**Question 8 (non-goals)** — A permanent “we will not claim / buy / hire X” list so nobody accidentally pitches Witching Hour as a reactor vendor or buys compliance theater.

**Question 13 (FedRAMP stages)** — Stage 0–1 = learn and document boundaries. Stage 2 = cheap engineering hygiene (logging, access control, evidence packs) without buying assessors. Stage 3+ = real authorization path with a sponsor/buyer.

**Dual license / public boundary** — Keeping some code or specs public (e.g. open source) while the company still owns the commercial rights federal agreements care about. We can unpack when you want.
